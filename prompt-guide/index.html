The formula

Four lines. That's the whole secret.

Here's the same request, asked two ways. Same AI. Completely different result.

Role+Context+Task+Format
The usual way
Write me a caption for my food post.
Delicious and nutritious! 😋 Enjoy this tasty bowl packed with flavor. Perfect for any occasion! #food #yummy #foodie
With the formula
Role: You are my social media bestie who writes in my voice: warm, playful, a little sassy.
Context: Chicken shawarma bowl reel. My audience is busy moms who love easy weeknight meals.
Task: A caption that makes them save the recipe.
Format: Under 80 words, end with a question.
Okay, these chicken shawarma bowls are about to save your Tuesday. 🙌 Juicy spiced chicken, golden turmeric rice, all on the table in 30 minutes. Save this one so you're not scrambling at 5:45. Be honest: what's your go-to busy-night dinner right now?
