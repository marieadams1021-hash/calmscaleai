Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this version made for loan officers?

Yes. When you check out, choose “Loan Officer” and you’ll get the mortgage version: the form questions, welcome email, NMLS signature lines, and guide are all written for loan officers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The Setup Guide walks you through every click with screenshots, and the one small script you'll use is copy-and-paste with clear instructions.

What does it cost to run each month?

It's built on free tools. Google is free, and Make's free plan covers roughly 250 leads a month. If you ever outgrow it, Make offers paid plans, which you'd pay Make for directly.

How fast do leads get the email?

Within seconds of hitting Submit. You get your CALL NOW alert at the same time.

I don't have a website. Can I still use it?

Yes. The kit builds you a simple inquiry form with a shareable link you can put in your bio, on a QR code, or in your email signature. Want a website too? That's the Done-For-You + Website package.

What happens with my website after launch?

It's yours. We build it and publish it in your own free hosting account (such as Netlify or GitHub Pages), so there's no monthly fee to us and nothing we need to keep running. We fix anything that isn't working as delivered for 14 days after launch. After that, changes can be requested as a separate one-time update, quoted before any work begins.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes. Buy it in your own account from any domain company (such as Cloudflare, Namecheap, or GoDaddy), and you'll renew it with them yourself. If it's ready before launch, we'll connect it for you, and you never share a password. You can also launch on your free web address and add a domain later.

Who owns the leads and the system?

You do. Everything lives in your own Google and Make accounts, and we never store your leads. Before using it alongside your day job, check your employment agreement and company policy on where lead information must be kept.

Is it compliant?

It's designed with good practices built in: a required permission checkbox, an opt-out line in every email, and a spot for your mailing address. You're still responsible for following your company's advertising rules and applicable laws, so check with your compliance team if you're unsure. This isn't legal advice.

Does it send text messages?

No, email only. Automated texting has stricter consent rules, so we kept it simple and email-based.

Can I get a refund?

The DIY Kit is a digital product delivered instantly, so all sales are final. If you don't receive it, or pick the wrong version, email us and we'll make it right. Setup packages have their own payment terms. See our Terms & Refund Policy for details.

What if I get stuck?

Reply to your kit delivery email and we'll point you in the right direction. If you'd rather have it done for you, you can move up to a Done-For-You package anytime.
