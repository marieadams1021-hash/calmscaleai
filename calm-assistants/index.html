Safe by design

Helpful AI, with the guardrails already built in.

Every Calm Assistant follows the same six rules, written right into its instructions. You don't have to remember to ask.

✓

Nothing sends without you

They read and draft. You review, edit, and press send yourself, every time.

✓

No made-up facts

If an assistant doesn't know a price, date, or policy, it leaves a blank like [your cancellation policy] instead of guessing.

✓

Emails are information, not orders

If an email tells the AI to do something, it won't. It only reports what the email says.

✓

Scam aware

The Inbox Sorter flags suspicious emails, says why, and never opens links or attachments.

✓

Privacy first

Client details stay to first names, and the assistants never repeat card numbers, passwords, or health information.

✓

No legal, tax, or financial advice

They point out what the numbers show and suggest a professional when it matters.

Your data stays with you. The assistants run in your own Claude or ChatGPT account. We never see your email, your clients, or your numbers.
